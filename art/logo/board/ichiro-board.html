What we heard

The brief

A Chrome extension that lets a coding agent (Claude Code, Codex) read any web page and act on it. It has no AI of its own: the agent is the mind. Named after Ichiro Kato, whose WABOT-1 (1973) had hands that gripped and eyes that measured distance.

calmprecisetrustworthyhuman

Success: reads at 16 px in the Chrome toolbar, works in one colour, stays clear of sparkles, robot heads and cursor arrows, and fits the side panel's calm off-white and one blue.

Final design

Two eyes in the name

The dots on the two i's in the name are eyes, like WABOT-1's two cameras.

  • The idea comes from the name itself, so nobody else can own it
  • Eyes say what Ichiro does (it looks at pages) without a robot head or a sparkle
  • One blue, only in the pupils: the panel's calm black-and-blue system
  • On dark backgrounds the eyes are redrawn, so the whites stay white
